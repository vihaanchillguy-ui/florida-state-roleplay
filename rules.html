KEEP IT RESPECTFUL

COMMUNITY RULES

These standards help keep Florida State Roleplay fair, welcoming and enjoyable for everyone. They apply to community spaces and organised sessions. Staff may publish additional instructions for particular events or departments, and the latest official rules in Discord take priority if anything here differs.

Please read the full official rules in the Discord server. Staff may provide additional instructions for specific sessions and departments.
01

Respect everyone

Treat members with patience and respect, even when you disagree. Harassment, discrimination, targeted insults, bullying and repeated attempts to provoke others damage the community and are not acceptable. Keep disagreements calm and take serious concerns to staff.

02

Follow staff directions

Session hosts and moderators help keep activities organised. Follow reasonable instructions, respect event boundaries and use the correct support channels when you need assistance. If you disagree with a decision, raise it calmly rather than disrupting the session.

03

Keep roleplay immersive

Help scenes feel believable by respecting the situation, avoiding unnecessary interruptions and giving other participants room to contribute. Follow any roleplay format or session instructions set by the host. Avoid disrupting other players' scenes.

04

Play fairly

Play honestly and give everyone a fair chance to participate. Exploiting, cheating, spamming, intentionally disrupting scenes or using unfair advantages undermines the experience and may lead to staff action.

05

Protect privacy

Keep personal details private. Do not post another member’s private information, share private conversations without permission or pressure anyone to reveal details about themselves. Report privacy concerns to staff.

06

Use common sense

Think before posting, use channels for their intended purpose and avoid behaviour that makes others feel unwelcome. Follow platform rules as well as community standards, and remember that your actions affect the whole group.

Have a question about a rule?

Ask the staff team through the official community Discord.
