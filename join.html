BECOME PART OF THE COMMUNITY

GET STARTED

Getting started is straightforward, but understanding the community makes your first session much better. Join the official Discord, read the current information, check which activities or departments are open and follow the instructions shared by the team. Recruitment and session details can change, so Discord is the best place to find accurate updates.

01

Join the Discord

Join through the official invitation and take a look around the server. Read the welcome information, check the channel descriptions and find where announcements, session updates and questions are posted.

02

Read the information

Before participating, read the community standards and current announcements. Look for session schedules, department recruitment notices and instructions from hosts or staff. If something is unclear, ask through the appropriate channel rather than guessing.

03

Get involved

When you are ready, follow the instructions for the session or department you are interested in. Listen to hosts, communicate respectfully, cooperate with other players and help make the experience enjoyable for everyone. Start by learning how the community works.

Your first stop is Discord.

Recruitment status, session times and department requirements can change. Check the official server for the latest details.
