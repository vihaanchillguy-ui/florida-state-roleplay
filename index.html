Community standards

These are starter guidelines. The official Discord rules and staff instructions always take priority.

01Respect every member. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable.
02Follow staff instructions and use the correct channels for questions or reports.
03Keep roleplay realistic and avoid disrupting other players' scenes.
04Do not exploit, cheat, spam or intentionally interfere with sessions.
05Keep personal information private, including your own and other members'.
06Represent the community responsibly in and outside organised sessions.
