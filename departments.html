THE DEPARTMENTS

Every department offers a different way to shape a session. Choose the roleplay area that suits your interests, work with other members and follow the guidance provided by session hosts. The descriptions below explain the general focus of each area; availability, recruitment, training and department-specific requirements are announced by the community team in Discord.

🚓
PUBLIC SAFETY

Law Enforcement

Take part in patrol-focused scenes, traffic interactions and coordinated public-safety operations. Members work together to create believable scenarios, communicate clearly and help keep roleplay organised. Follow the session host directions and department guidance shared in Discord.

🚒
EMERGENCY SERVICES

Fire & Rescue

Bring emergency-response stories to life through fire-related incidents, rescue scenarios and scene coordination. This roleplay area focuses on teamwork, clear communication and supporting other participants as a situation develops.

🚑
EMERGENCY SERVICES

Emergency Medical

Take part in medical-response storylines, patient-care interactions and coordinated emergency scenes. The focus is on respectful, believable roleplay and working alongside other departments to help each scenario flow smoothly.

🚗
COMMUNITY ROLEPLAY

Civilian Operations

Build the everyday side of Liberty County through civilian characters, businesses, road interactions and community storylines. Civilian roleplay gives sessions variety and helps create situations that make a shared world feel active and believable.

📡
SESSION SUPPORT

Communications

Help keep sessions connected by sharing information, supporting coordination and communicating updates clearly. This area suits members who enjoy organisation and helping participants stay informed during community activities.

★
COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Staff Team

Support the community by welcoming members, answering questions and helping maintain a respectful environment. Staff responsibilities and recruitment are managed by the leadership team, with expectations and openings communicated through official channels.

Interested in a department?

Check the Discord for recruitment announcements and current requirements.
