KEEP IT RESPECTFUL

COMMUNITY RULES

These are the website's core community standards. Any official rules posted by Florida State Roleplay staff in Discord take priority.

Please read the full official rules in the Discord server. Staff may provide additional instructions for specific sessions and departments.
01

Respect everyone

Treat all members fairly. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable.

02

Follow staff directions

Listen to session hosts and moderators, and use the correct channels when you need help.

03

Keep roleplay immersive

Stay in character where appropriate and avoid disrupting other players' scenes.

04

Play fairly

Do not exploit, cheat, spam or intentionally interfere with community sessions.

05

Protect privacy

Do not share private information about yourself or another member.

06

Use common sense

Help keep the server welcoming and represent the community responsibly.

Have a question about a rule?

Ask the staff team through the official community Discord.
